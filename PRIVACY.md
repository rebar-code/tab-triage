# Tab Triage privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Tab Triage does not collect, sell or share your data. It has no servers, no analytics and no tracking.

## What it reads

- **Your open tabs:** titles, URLs and when you last viewed them, to group, sort and close them.
- **github.com pages:** for open GitHub pull request and issue tabs, it loads the page from github.com with your existing GitHub session to read its state (open, merged, closed, draft).
- **Page text, only when you ask:** a filter that matches text on a page reads that page in your browser. Chrome asks you to grant access to the site first.

## What it stores

Settings, saved tabs, filters and cached PR states are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

## What leaves your computer

Nothing, except the requests to github.com described above. Those go only to GitHub, as if you had reloaded the tab.
